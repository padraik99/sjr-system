Morning — post-walk, warm

  1. Dead hangs 30–60s × 2–3 — decompress first
  2. Prone lying 3–5 min — pillow under chest
  3. Prone press-ups ×10, 1–2 sets — hips stay down
  4. Sciatic glide 5 / side — tension not pain; stop if calf/ankle ↑; left cautious
  5. Standing extensions ×10

Through the day

  • Standing extensions ×10 every 1–2 hrs
  • Tingling → stand immediately + 10 standing extensions
  • Position/pacing cycling is normal flare behavior — not a failure

Afternoon — ~4pm, before the wall

  • NexWave TENS 20–25 min (HMD mode) → prone lying → standing ext ×10
  • Log before/after pain (/10) — this is Kataria's data

Evening — 30–45 min pre-bed

  • Prone press-ups ×8–10, reduced ROM
  • Diaphragmatic breathing, prone ×10 — 4 in / hold 2 / 6 out
  • Transition straight to sleep position

Canal walk — daily

  • 2 miles. First mile easy, forward.
  • Backwards intervals ×3 (pole-to-pole), well spaced
  • 30s forward-gait reset after each interval
  • Hold the line: no added duration/sets/intensity until 1 full week of non-response

Traffic light — governs every session

●Green — leg pain same or reduced → continue. Nerve root is tolerating the load.
●Yellow — back pain up, leg pain unchanged → OK, log it. Symptoms moving toward the spine is the direction you want (centralizing — judge by direction, not intensity).
●Red — leg pain increases → STOP. Peripheralization overrides everything. Back off, note the trigger, don't repeat that variable tomorrow.

Not this phase (Phase 2 — locked until centralization)

No flexion · no knee-to-chest · no piriformis or hamstring stretching · no core/stabilization · no hip strengthening.
