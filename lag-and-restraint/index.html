The Scalding Shower

You turn the tap. Nothing happens. So you turn it more. The hot water was already coming — it just hadn't reached you yet. Drag the sliders and watch the same shower go from comfortable to hunting to scald-and-freeze.

how long the water takes to reach your skin
how hard you chase the temperature you feel
—

Turning up reaction strength when the delay is long doesn't fix the shower — it's what breaks it. There are only two ways out: shorten the lag, or soften the response. That second option has a name. It's restraint.

Restraint is respect for what you can't yet see.
